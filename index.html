About Us

Coolness Interactive was founded by the two teens: guahlg and Walnutty in the Spring of 2025, as a small group of developers creating games and projects together. The name of the studio was playfully named Coolness Interactive. Other members of the studio today include El Ocelote, DragonDev22 and Restitutor Orbis. Our discord server houses a community of like minded tech-interested people and hosts regular events to bring our members closer together! Projects we've worked on are Project Voidmarch, which was discontinued December 2025, and Bertmania, finished February 2026. Currently our studio is developing the game Project Watt Cubed, a sandbox voxel game that you can get a peek of below. We have a youtube channel: Coolness Studio, where we occasionally post updates and videos. Our community also has projects to show, just scroll down below to find them! See you in our discord server!
